\TCUHeadingTwo{Academic Conduct}
The Computer Science Department takes academic dishonesty quite seriously. Academic misconduct will not be tolerated. Such acts are detailed in the current TCU Academic Catalog and include: copying, using, or in any way misrepresenting another's work as your own; substituting for another or having someone substitute for you; plagiarism; collusion; abusing resource materials; unauthorized use of computer software or hardware; fabrication and falsification; complicity in misconduct. Such conduct at a minimum results in a zero on the test or assignment, and may result in a failing grade for the course.

\TCUHeadingTwo{Late Work Policy}
Unless an extension or accommodation applies, work submitted up to 24 hours late receives a deduction of 15\% of the points earned; work more than 24 but no more than 48 hours late receives a deduction of 30\% of the points earned. Work more than 48 hours late is not accepted without an approved arrangement. Weekends and holidays count toward elapsed time.

\TCUHeadingTwo{Absence/Make-up Policy}
Regular attendance and participation are expected for all class meetings and activities. If you must miss class due to illness, family emergency, or a university-sponsored event, please notify the instructor as soon as possible---preferably before the absence occurs. Documentation may be required for excused absences.

Students are responsible for making up any missed work, assignments, or exams. Make-up exams or assignments must be arranged with the instructor within one week of the missed class unless prior arrangements have been made. Unexcused absences may result in a grade penalty or loss of make-up privileges.

The inappropriate or unauthorized use of AI-generated content may constitute academic misconduct. Misuse of AI or other assignment-help tools will be handled according to TCU's Academic Conduct Policy and may result in sanctions including failing the course.
