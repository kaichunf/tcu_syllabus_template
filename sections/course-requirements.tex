\TCUHeadingThree{Participation, Engagement \& Attendance}
Active participation, consistent engagement, and regular attendance are essential components of student success in this course. Students are expected to contribute thoughtfully to class discussions, collaborate respectfully with peers, and complete assigned activities during class sessions. Absences should be communicated in advance whenever possible, and it is the student's responsibility to catch up on any missed material or assignments. Repeated unexcused absences or lack of engagement may negatively impact your participation grade and overall performance in the course.
